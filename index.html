Mates’ Support — EAP

Free, confidential support for work or home stress — for you and your mates.

It’s OK to reach out 💬

Talking to someone helps — whether it’s pressure on the job, money, family, sleep, or just not feeling yourself. EAP counsellors are independent and confidential; your employer isn’t told who calls. Looking out for your mates matters too — if someone seems off, ask how they’re going.
